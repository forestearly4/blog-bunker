Connecting Facebook & Instagram

A step-by-step guide to setting up a Meta app so Blog Bunker can publish directly to your Facebook Page and Instagram account.

Why is this needed? Meta requires every app that publishes on someone's behalf — not just Blog Bunker — to be registered through their developer platform. There's no shortcut around this step; it's a one-time setup, not something you'll need to repeat.

1 Create the app

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps and click Create App.
  2. Choose Business as the app type.
  3. Give it a name (e.g. "Blog Bunker") and your contact email, then select or create a Business Portfolio — your own is fine.

2 Add Facebook Login for Business

In the left sidebar, find Use cases and select or customize "Manage everything on your Page" (or add Facebook Login for Business directly if you don't see a use-case picker).

Inside that product's settings, find Valid OAuth Redirect URIs and add exactly this:

https://blogbunker.netlify.app/api/meta-callback
This must match exactly — no trailing slash, no trailing space. A mismatch here is the most common reason a connection silently fails.

3 Add Instagram publishing

Still under Use cases, find and add "Manage everything on Instagram". Inside it, choose "API setup with Facebook Login" — not "with Instagram Login" — since Blog Bunker connects through a Facebook Page with a linked Instagram account.

Click "Add all required permissions" for content management. Then confirm the full list matches what Blog Bunker actually requests:

pages_show_list pages_manage_posts pages_read_engagement instagram_basic instagram_content_publish business_management

If pages_manage_posts isn't included in the auto-added bundle, add it manually — this is a common gap.

4 Add your Facebook Page to the app's Business Portfolio

This step trips people up more than any other, so don't skip it even if your Page already exists.

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and make sure you're in the same Business Portfolio your app is tied to.
  2. Go to Business Settings → Accounts → Pages, click Add, then Add a Page.
  3. Search for your Page and add it. You'll need Admin access to it on your Facebook account.
If your Page doesn't appear when connecting in Blog Bunker, this is almost always why — the Page hasn't been added to the app's Business Portfolio yet, or your account only has Editor (not Admin) access to it.

5 Make sure your Instagram account can be linked

The Instagram account needs to be a Business or Creator account (not personal), linked to the Facebook Page from step 4. If it's currently a personal account, convert it under Instagram's own Settings first, then link it from the Facebook Page's own Settings → Instagram.

6 Add yourself (and testers) as Testers

While the app is in Development Mode — which is fine for a beta — anyone connecting needs to be added under App roles → Roles as a Tester. Add each beta tester's Facebook account by name; they'll get a notification to accept before they can connect.

You don't need to submit for full App Review to run a beta this way — Testers can connect and publish immediately once accepted.

7 Connect in Blog Bunker

Back in Blog Bunker, go to Settings → Facebook & Instagram → Connect. If your account has access to more than one Page, Blog Bunker will ask you to pick which one this specific workspace should publish to — each workspace remembers its own choice independently.

Common questions

My Page isn't showing up in the connection screen.

Almost always a Business Portfolio issue — see step 4. Double-check the Page has actually been added and approved in the same portfolio your app is tied to, and that you have Admin (not just Editor) access to it.

Can I use one Meta app for multiple workspaces or brands?

Yes. The app itself is just the underlying credential — it's not tied to a specific Page. You can connect a different Page for each workspace using the same app; Blog Bunker keeps each workspace's chosen Page separate.

Do I need to publish my app publicly on Meta's App Review?

Not for a small beta. Adding people individually as Testers (step 6) lets them connect and publish without your app needing to pass full App Review. You'd only need that for opening the connection up to the general public.

I connected, but posts aren't showing up on Instagram — only Facebook.

Check that the Instagram account is a Business/Creator account properly linked to the Page you selected (step 5), and that instagram_content_publish is actually listed among your app's granted permissions (step 3).

Still stuck? Use the Report a Bug button inside Blog Bunker and describe where you got stuck — that's the fastest way to get help.
