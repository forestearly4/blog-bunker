Blog Bunker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Blog Bunker ("we," "us," "our") provides a blogging and content-operations dashboard for bloggers and small publishers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it's protected — including when you connect a third-party account such as Pinterest, WordPress, or Google Search Console.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and basic profile info when you sign up.
  • Content you create: blog posts, drafts, images, and related metadata you generate or upload within Blog Bunker.
  • Connected account tokens: when you connect a third-party service (Pinterest, WordPress, Google Search Console, Meta/Instagram, etc.), we store the access credentials needed to act on your behalf — for example, to publish a pin or a blog post at your request.

How We Use Third-Party Connections

When you connect Pinterest (or any other integration), Blog Bunker only takes actions you explicitly initiate — such as creating a board or publishing a pin from a specific post. We do not post, follow, message, or modify your account automatically or without your action.

  • We request only the permissions (scopes) required to provide this functionality — reading and creating boards and pins, and reading your basic account info to confirm the connection.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your connected-account data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • You can disconnect any third-party account at any time from Blog Bunker's Settings page, which revokes our stored access token.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit. Access credentials for connected accounts are stored securely and are never displayed in full within the app after initial connection.

Data Retention & Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Disconnecting a third-party integration removes the associated stored credentials.
